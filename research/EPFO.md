# EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund Organisation) — Research Brief

Compiled 2026-08-24 for "Build What Moves India" hackathon platform-selection. Platform: EPFO / Unified Member Portal (unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in), EPFiGMS grievance portal.

---

## 1. What the platform actually does

EPFO runs the compulsory retirement savings scheme for India's formal-sector salaried workforce (~7+ crore active subscribers). The member-facing flows on the Unified Member Portal are:

- **PF final withdrawal / advance claim (Form 19 / Form 31 / Form 10C)** — a member files a claim against their UAN (Universal Account Number) for full settlement (on leaving service) or partial advance (medical, housing, education, marriage, unemployment). Since May 2024 this no longer requires a cheque-leaf/passbook image upload ([EPFO press release](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/EPFO_PRESS_RELEASES/EPO_SimplifiesClaimSettlementProcess.pdf)).
- **PF transfer claim (Form 13 / OTCP — Online Transfer Claim Portal)** — moves accumulated balance from a previous employer's PF account to the current one when a member changes jobs; needs matching member IDs, KYC, and service-history continuity ([EPFO OTCP FAQ](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/OTCP_PDFs/MembersFAQ.pdf)).
- **UAN activation / KYC seeding** — linking Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account to the UAN; a prerequisite gate for almost every other claim.
- **EPS (Employees' Pension Scheme) claims** — Form 10C (withdrawal benefit) or Form 10D (monthly pension) at retirement/exit, evaluated against EPS-specific eligibility rules (minimum 10 years' pensionable service, correct EPS contribution history) separate from the EPF balance itself.
- **Claim status tracking** — "Track Claim Status" screen on the member portal, showing a claim as pending/approved/rejected, historically with terse or coded rejection reasons rather than plain-language explanations.
- **Grievance redressal** — EPFiGMS (EPF internet Grievance Management System), a separate portal a member must proactively use to escalate a stuck or rejected claim; distinct system from the claim tracker itself.

## 2. Where it concretely fails users today

### Rejection rate is high and trending worse
- **26% of all claims were rejected in 2023-24** — 160 lakh of 623 lakh claims received; ~20 lakh were still pending. The **average rejection rate for 2019-20 to 2023-24 was 27%**, up from an average of **17% in 2014-15 to 2018-19** — a documented, worsening trend, not a one-off. Source: [Dataful / EPFO annual report analysis](https://insights.dataful.in/articles/epfo-claim-rejection-rate-at-26-in-2023-24), corroborated by Deccan Herald's "one in three EPF final settlement claims rejected" report (title indicates a similar or higher rejection framing for final settlements specifically; full article body was not accessible — flagged as **directionally consistent but not independently re-verified** since the source page returned HTTP 403 on fetch).

### Grievances have grown sixfold in a decade
- EPFiGMS grievances rose from **2,48,072 in 2012-13 to 16,14,386 in 2023-24** — a 6.5x increase over 11 years. Source: [Dataful](https://insights.dataful.in/articles/epfo-claim-rejection-rate-at-26-in-2023-24).
- For **FY2024-25**, EPFO reported **over 16 lakh grievances** on EPFiGMS and **over 1.74 lakh** on the government-wide CPGRAMS portal, claiming 98% were "redressed within timelines" — a metric that measures response speed, not whether the underlying problem was actually resolved. Source: [News on Air, June 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/epfo-urges-members-to-use-portal-for-free-secure-online-services).
- **Nearly half of all grievances cluster in four categories**: non-transfer of PF accumulations, final PF withdrawal issues, KYC problems at the PF office, and non-settlement of PF advance claims — i.e., the core claim-processing pipeline itself, not peripheral features. Source: [Dataful](https://insights.dataful.in/articles/epfo-claim-rejection-rate-at-26-in-2023-24).

### The ~6 recurring rejection reasons (converging across multiple sources)
1. **UAN–Aadhaar personal-detail mismatch** — name, DOB, or gender differs even slightly between UAN/EPFO records and Aadhaar; a single-letter difference triggers automatic rejection. ([Kustodian.life](https://kustodian.life/resources/epf-claim-rejected-name-aadhaar-dob-mismatch-fix-guide-2025), [PensionBazaar](https://www.pensionbazaar.com/epf/claim-rejected/))
2. **Missing/incorrect date of exit** — previous employer never marked the member's exit date on the portal, so the system cannot process a claim on that account. ([Employnova](https://employnova.net/epfo-claim/))
3. **Employer non-attestation / approval delay** — several claim types still route through employer digital sign-off before EPFO will act; delay or refusal by the employer stalls the claim indefinitely with no escalation path visible to the employee. ([Kotak Life](https://www.kotaklife.com/insurance-guide/retirement/epfo-claim-rejected-reason), [NewsX](https://www.newsx.com/business/epf-claim-rejected-here-are-the-most-common-reasons-and-step-by-step-guide-on-how-to-fix-them-132191/))
4. **KYC not verified** — Aadhaar/PAN/bank details uploaded but not "verified" status in EPFO's system (a distinct state from merely "linked"); withdrawal and transfer claims are blocked until this flips. ([Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/investments/epf-claim-rejection-reasons))
5. **Service overlap between two employers** — EPFO's records show overlapping employment dates across two establishments (usually because a previous employer never closed out the exit date), flagging the claim for manual review or rejection. **Reform in 2025**: EPFO reportedly changed policy so overlap alone can no longer be sole grounds for rejection, and minor date mismatches from tech glitches are meant to be waved through — but member-facing guides as late as 2026 still list it as an active rejection cause, suggesting inconsistent rollout. ([Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/varsity/epfo-claim-rejections-what-is-service-overlap-and-how-to-merge-pf-accounts), [Kustodian.life](https://kustodian.life/resources/epf-overlap-issues-fix-date-of-exit-joining-errors-that-delay-your-claim))
6. **Member ID / establishment code errors** — wrong establishment code, extension, or regional office code embedded in a transfer claim (Form 13/OTCP) causes automatic rejection at a pure data-matching level. ([CitizenNest](https://www.citizennest.com/guide/pf-transfer-online-not-working-fix))

### Claims sit stuck for weeks with no visible cause
- EPFO's own field officers wrote formally to the Labour Minister (reported **August 2026**) warning that **many PF claims have remained pending beyond 20 days** — the portal's own SLA threshold — due to under-resourced IT infrastructure (CITES — Centralised IT Enabled System). Source: [BusinessToday, Aug 5, 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/news/story/pf-claim-delays-epfo-officers-flag-cites-glitches-urge-labour-minister-to-boost-tech-team-547304-2026-08-05), [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/business%2Fepfo-officers-write-to-minister-raise-concern-over-inadequate-it-infra-3113834).
- The same letter states there has been **no direct recruitment into EPFO's Information Services Division since 2004**, several senior technical staff retired/resigned unreplaced, no full-time CTO for three years, and the last member-workload assessment was done in **2016-17** despite the subscriber base growing sharply since. This is a structural, backend capacity failure, not a UI problem.

### Historical audit findings (context, not current-year)
- A 2019 internal EPFO audit found accounts with **negative/adverse balances**, some exceeding Rs 1 lakh, attributed to possible fraud or accounting errors. Source: [Business Standard, Sept 2019](https://www.business-standard.com/article/economy-policy/epfo-audit-shows-serious-gaps-in-management-of-provident-fund-accounts-119092701543_1.html).
- An older CAG performance audit found Rs 313.20 crore recoverable in EPF arrears from 20,974 establishments and inspection shortfalls versus prescribed targets — evidence of longstanding weak backend controls, though this data is over a decade old and should be flagged as **historical context**, not current-state evidence. Source: [CAG report PDF, 2012 cycle](https://cag.gov.in/uploads/download_audit_report/2013/Union_Performance_Ministry_Labour_and_Employment_32_2013.pdf).

## 3. Root causes — backend/process, not UI

This maps cleanly onto the hackathon's "fix the backend, not the screen" requirement:

- **Opaque rejection communication is a process design choice.** The claim status screen historically returns short codes or terse text rather than plain-language explanation of exactly which field mismatched and what document would fix it. Every "how to fix a rejected EPF claim" guide found in this research exists precisely because EPFO's own system doesn't tell the member that.
- **Employer-in-the-loop attestation is a structural bottleneck.** For certain claim types, the process requires an employer's digital sign-off with no SLA enforcement or escalation path visible to the employee if the employer simply doesn't act. EPFO's own April 2025 reform ("Removal of Employer Approval... benefiting 14.95 lakh pending cases") is an admission that this was a real, sizeable structural blocker, not a perception problem. Source: [EPFO press release](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/EPFO_PRESS_RELEASES/EPO_SimplifiesClaimSettlementProcess.pdf).
- **KYC seeding pipeline has a "linked vs verified" state gap** that isn't surfaced to the member until a claim is filed and rejected — a validation step that could run earlier in the flow, before submission, is currently discovered only after failure.
- **Multi-employer identity fragmentation** (service overlap, multiple UANs, uncleared exit dates) is a data-integrity problem inherited from employers not updating records — EPFO's system currently pushes the burden of detecting and resolving this onto the individual member after rejection, rather than surfacing it proactively.
- **IT/staffing capacity is the deepest root cause** per EPFO's own officers: automation was supposed to reduce settlement time, but a short-staffed, un-modernized backend (CITES) is the actual bottleneck behind claims stuck past the 20-day SLA.

## 4. What's already been tried

- **Auto-claim settlement expansion**: auto-mode settlements grew from 89.52 lakh (FY2023-24) to **2.34 crore in FY2024-25** (a 161% increase), with the auto-settlement limit raised from Rs 1 lakh to **Rs 5 lakh** in June 2025, processing eligible claims within 3 days. Sources: [IHRC](https://ihrcgroup.com/blog/EPFO-Enhances-Auto-Settlement-24th-June-2025), [News on Air, Feb 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/epfo-achieves-milestone-of-over-5-crore-claim-settlements-in-fy-2024-25-minister-mansukh-mandaviya).
- **Documentation simplification** (May 2024): removed cheque-leaf/passbook image upload requirement, benefiting 1.7 crore members and cutting a documented cause of rejection (poor-quality uploads). Source: [EPFO press release](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/EPFO_PRESS_RELEASES/EPO_SimplifiesClaimSettlementProcess.pdf).
- **Employer-approval removal** (April 2025) for a subset of claim types, immediately unblocking 14.95 lakh pending cases.
- **Overlap-rejection policy softening** (2025): overlap alone reportedly no longer sufficient grounds for automatic rejection.
- **Gap that remains despite these reforms**: none of the above reforms address (a) plain-language explanation of *why* a specific claim was rejected, (b) a pre-submission readiness check that catches a mismatch before filing rather than after, or (c) a way for a member to resolve service overlap / multi-UAN merge themselves without guessing which form applies. The rejection rate data (26% in 2023-24) reflects the same year these reforms were rolling out — meaning even with reform momentum, the front-line failure-explanation gap is unaddressed. And the August 2026 officers' letter shows the IT-capacity root cause is *still* unresolved as of the most recent reporting.

## 5. Buildathon fit assessment

**Verdict: moderate fit, with a real risk of feeling like a static rules-lookup — Varun's own concern is well-founded and should be taken seriously.**

- **What's easy to mock**: a synthetic claim dataset (UAN, employer records, KYC state, claim type, submission date) mapped to the ~6 known rejection reasons above is fully buildable without touching any live system — no Aadhaar/PAN/OTP/payment data needed, satisfying the hard rules cleanly.
- **What makes it risky**: EPFO's actual rejection logic is close to a deterministic rules engine (does UAN name match Aadhaar name? Is exit date set? Is employer attestation flag true?). A tool that just re-implements those rules against mock records **is** a rules-lookup — closer to a form validator than a "backend/process fix." The judges are explicitly looking for something that changes the *process*, not a smarter error message.
- **How to strengthen it if chosen**: the stronger framing is not "diagnose why claim X was rejected" (that's the rules-lookup trap) but **"catch the mismatch before submission and generate the exact fix artifact"** — e.g., a pre-flight checker that cross-references a user's self-reported details against their UAN/Aadhaar/employer record *before* they file, flags the specific field, and auto-drafts the correction request (to the employer for exit-date/attestation issues, or a joint-declaration form for name mismatches) rather than just describing the problem. This reframes the build around the actual process gap EPFO's own reforms have been chasing (employer bottleneck, KYC verification gap) instead of restating rejection codes in plain English.
- **Comparison to Varun's other idea (SIR/voter-roll)**: that idea has a genuine "resolver" mechanic (address → ward/constituency, ambiguous today) that's harder to reduce to a lookup table. EPFO's claim-diagnosis idea is more mockable but has a shallower "wow" ceiling in a live demo unless the pre-flight/auto-draft angle above is built in.

## 6. Concrete "fix the failing moment" problem statement candidates

> **"My PF claim was rejected and the portal won't tell me why in words I understand."** A salaried worker files a Form 19 final-settlement claim after leaving a job, waits weeks, and gets back a status of "rejected" with a terse code — not a sentence explaining that their Aadhaar middle name doesn't match their UAN record, or that their former employer never entered an exit date. This is not a rare edge case: EPFO's own data shows a **26% rejection rate in 2023-24** (160 lakh of 623 lakh claims), up from an average of 17% pre-2019, and "final PF withdrawal" and "KYC issue at PF office" sit among the top four causes behind a **sixfold rise in grievances** (2.48 lakh in 2012-13 to 16.1 lakh in 2023-24) ([Dataful](https://insights.dataful.in/articles/epfo-claim-rejection-rate-at-26-in-2023-24)). Strong buildathon fit if scoped as a pre-submission check rather than a post-rejection explainer: a synthetic dataset of UAN/Aadhaar/employer records mapped to the 6 known mismatch types, checked *before* a mock claim is filed, catching the failure before it happens rather than decoding it after — this is the backend/process fix, not a reskinned status page.

> **"My employer won't sign off, and there's nothing I can do about it."** Several EPFO claim types require the previous employer's digital attestation before the claim moves forward, and until April 2025 there was no mechanism to bypass a non-responsive employer — EPFO's own reform removing employer approval for a subset of claims **immediately unblocked 14.95 lakh pending cases**, which is itself the evidence of how large and real this bottleneck was ([EPFO press release](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/EPO_SimplifiesClaimSettlementProcess.pdf)). The reform did not cover every claim type, and there's still no member-facing tool that tells someone "your claim is stuck because your employer hasn't acted, here's the escalation path and the exact grievance to file, with a countdown to your SLA breach." Good buildathon fit: mock an employer-attestation queue with time-in-queue data, and build the diagnostic-plus-auto-drafted-escalation layer EPFO doesn't currently offer.

> **"Why has my claim just been sitting there for a month?"** EPFO's own field officers wrote to the Labour Minister in **August 2026** warning that claims are routinely stuck past the portal's 20-day SLA because of an under-resourced backend IT system (CITES) — no direct hiring into the technical division since 2004, no full-time CTO for three years, and a workload assessment last done in 2016-17 ([BusinessToday, Aug 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/news/story/pf-claim-delays-epfo-officers-flag-cites-glitches-urge-labour-minister-to-boost-tech-team-547304-2026-08-05)). A member has no way today to know whether their specific claim is stuck for a legitimate document reason or purely stuck in a systemic backlog. Weaker buildathon fit as a standalone idea — it demos as a status dashboard rather than a moment being fixed for one person — but is strong supporting evidence for why any "explain my claim" tool needs a "this delay is on you vs. this delay is on the system" distinction built in.

> **"My PF transfer never happened and I don't know which of my two UANs it's stuck between."** Job changers frequently end up with a duplicate UAN or an unresolved service-overlap flag because a previous employer never closed out their exit date; EPFO's OTCP transfer claims fail on member-ID mismatches, unmerged UANs, and overlap flags, and "non-transfer of PF accumulations" is EPFO's single largest grievance category ([Dataful](https://insights.dataful.in/articles/epfo-claim-rejection-rate-at-26-in-2023-24), [Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/varsity/epfo-claim-rejections-what-is-service-overlap-and-how-to-merge-pf-accounts)). EPFO's 2025 policy change says overlap alone should no longer block a claim, but member guides as recent as 2026 still list it as an active rejection cause — suggesting the rule change hasn't reliably reached the processing layer. Moderate buildathon fit: a synthetic multi-UAN, multi-employer service-history dataset lets a tool detect the overlap, tell the user in plain terms which UAN is "live," and generate the correct merge form (Form 13) — genuinely useful, but risks feeling like a diagnostic lookup unless it also auto-drafts the merge request.

> **"Is my pension claim actually going to qualify, or am I about to find out after I've already left my job?"** EPS (pension) claims fail for reasons distinct from ordinary PF withdrawal — under 10 years of pensionable service, missing EPS contribution history, or unverified inter-employer transfers — and these failures surface only *after* a member has already exited employment and filed Form 10C/10D, when there is no way back to fix an incomplete contribution record ([Kustodian.life](https://kustodian.life/resources/epf-claim-rejected-because-of-eps-top-reasons-and-proven-fixes-india-2026-guide)). This is arguably the highest-stakes failure mode on the platform because the moment it's discovered is irreversible. Good buildathon fit as a "pre-flight for pension eligibility" — a mock service-history and EPS-contribution dataset lets the tool tell someone, while they're still employed, exactly which gap (service years, contribution record, unmerged UAN) would sink their eventual pension claim, giving them time to fix it before it's too late. This is the strongest "fix the process, not the screen" framing of the set: it moves the intervention point from after-the-fact rejection to a moment where correction is still possible.
