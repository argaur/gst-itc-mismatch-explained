# MCA / MCA21 — Primary Research (real user/practitioner voices)

Companion to `research/MCA.md` (secondary research, director-disqualification-dashboard lead). This file does NOT duplicate that — it captures what actual founders, first-time directors, and CS/CA practitioners are saying in their own words: forums, article comment sections, social media, and trade press quoting named professionals.

**Honest finding on method:** MCA21 does not have the Reddit/Twitter density that IRCTC, EPFO, or the Income Tax portal have. Multiple targeted Reddit searches (r/india, r/IndianStartUps, r/developersIndia, r/IndiaTax — `site:reddit.com MCA21`, `SPICe+ rejected reddit`, `DIN DSC problem reddit`, `director disqualification reddit`) returned **no indexed Reddit threads**. This itself is a signal (see Signal strength note). The real complaint trail for MCA21 lives in: (1) CA/CS professional community sites (CAclubindia, TaxGuru, Taxscan) where practitioners post on behalf of client founders, (2) the official @MCA21India / @HelpdeskMCA21V3 X account and its replies, and (3) trade press (Inc42, Taxscan) that directly quotes named CAs and CS professionals reacting to portal failures. Where a quote is from a named professional describing what happened to their client/company (not their personal ROC filing), that is noted.

## Cluster 1: MCA21 V3 portal bugs / migration chaos
**Strongest-evidenced cluster.** V2→V3 migration (ongoing since 2023, still causing new pain in Sept 2025) is the single most complained-about failure mode, and complaints come from named professionals, not anonymous handles.

- **Source:** Taxscan, "MCA V3 Portal Glitches: ICSI Urges MCA to Extend Filing Deadlines & Waive Additional Fee for Sept–Dec Forms" — https://www.taxscan.in/top-stories/mca-v-portal-glitches-icsi-urges-mca-to-extend-filing-deadlines-waive-additional-fee-for-sept-dec-forms-1434949
  - **Date:** ICSI letter dated 29 Sept 2025, reported Oct 2025.
  - **Named source:** CS Dhananjay Shukla, ICSI President, in a formal letter to MCA (representing thousands of practicing CS members, i.e. aggregated real practitioner pain, not one voice).
  - **Quote:** "the transition from MCA-21 V2 to V3, while deployed with the intention to improve ease of doing business, has inadvertently left professionals unable to file numerous e-forms."
  - **What went wrong (practitioner's own framing):** DIR-3 WebKYC OTP generation fails for foreign nationals; IVR OTP backup also broken. Prefilled form data is wrong (e.g. RUN name-approval data populating incorrectly). Forms throw "Validation Error: Submission Restricted" with no way to proceed. Approved filings (PAS-3, CRF) don't reflect in MCA's own master data — i.e. you file, it's accepted, and the system still shows the old (wrong) state. DSC affixed correctly still throws "DSC Verification Failed." "Forgot Password" throws a generic "Something went wrong." Document downloads pull the wrong company's forms or only the attachment, not the form itself.
  - **Ask from the profession:** extend deadlines to 31 Dec 2025 and waive late fees for Sept–Dec 2025 filings — i.e. practitioners are asking the government to formally acknowledge the portal, not the filer, is at fault.

- **Source:** TaxGuru, "Common issues/concerns/technical glitches on MCA-21 V3 Portal" (compiling an ICSI representation letter + reader comments) — https://taxguru.in/chartered-accountant/common-issues-concerns-technical-glitches-mca-21-v3-portal.html
  - Underlying ICSI letter dated **29 March 2023** — shows this has been a running complaint for 2.5+ years, not a one-off.
  - **Real reader comment, "Priyanka", 26 July 2023:** "I am unable to download Certificate of Incorporation for LLP for last one month. I get repeatedly the same message: technical error." — i.e. an LLP that is legally incorporated cannot get the document proving it, for a full month.
  - **Real reader comment, "Meenu Gupta", 12 May 2023:** reported a "Personal details mismatch" error while trying to register as a business user against her own DIN — blocked from even logging in to file anything.
  - **Real reader comment, "SANYA", 8 June 2023:** independently reported the same "Personal details mismatch" error a month later, asking if anyone had a fix — shows the bug persisted unfixed for weeks with practitioners crowdsourcing a workaround MCA hadn't published.
  - **23 numbered issue categories** in the ICSI letter span incorporation forms, compliance filings, login, and data sync — this is a portal with systemic reliability problems, not an isolated bug.

- **Source:** Inc42, "Tech Glitches On MCA Portal Halting Startup Filings" — https://inc42.com/buzz/tech-glitches-halts-startup-filings-on-mca-site/
  - **Devansh Lakhani**, CA and angel investor: "I believe this issue might take some time to resolve and MCA should go back to V2 forms till the time it's not resolved." — a practitioner recommending the government roll back its own flagship digitisation project.
  - **Nakul Grover**, Partner, Cicero Chambers LLP: "Even after the issuance of the circular [waiving penalties], the MCA21 portal is not waiving off penalties." — i.e. MCA announced relief, but the portal itself did not implement it, so filers were still charged.
  - **Jitender Ahlawat**, Founder, HJA & Associates LLP: "Incorporation form (INC-7) which is used for incorporation of a new company is not available in the V3 version" — during the transition window, a core incorporation form was simply missing from the new system, stalling new company registrations entirely. Also: PAS-3 form (return of allotment) couldn't have certain columns amended, blocking filings outright with no workaround.

- **Source:** MCA's own official account acknowledging the problem publicly — Ministry of Corporate Affairs (@MCA21India) on X, https://x.com/MCA21India/status/1937425524944503167 (June 2025) and https://x.com/MCA21India/status/1668257947556642820 (2023): "The consultations will be open to all where stakeholders can raise specific issues regarding the MCA-21 portal. The Ministry along with @LTIMindtreeOFCL will strive towards resolution of the issues by June 30, 2023." — the government itself set (and implicitly missed/rolled-forward) a fix deadline, and stood up a dedicated complaint handle (@HelpdeskMCA21V3) specifically because volume of stakeholder complaints on the main handle was high enough to need routing.

## Cluster 2: Incorporation (SPICe+) rejected or stuck, cause unclear to the filer
- Rejection reasons compiled from professional sources (Razorpay Rize FAQ, Globaton) show the pattern: name similarity, DSC-PAN mismatch, address proof >2 months old (or utility bill >60 days old — sources disagree on exact cutoff, itself a sign the rule is inconsistently understood even by professionals), NOC not on stamp paper, object clause mismatch with company name, registered-office state in MOA not matching the state picked in the form.
- **Structural failure mode documented by Globaton** ("Why SPICe+ Part B Rejections Hurt Twice"): if SPICe+ Part B is rejected, the DIN applications bundled inside the same form are **automatically rejected too** — so a single clerical mismatch costs the filer both the company name reservation and the director ID, forcing a full fresh Part A refiling with fresh fees. This is a compounding-failure UX problem: one error cascades into losing everything already submitted.
- This cluster is well-documented by professional service firms (who clearly get this question constantly from clients) but we did not find a first-person founder quote in the time available — flagged as a gap, see Signal strength.

## Cluster 3: DIN / DSC activation friction
- **Source:** TaxGuru, "MCA Advisory: Fix 'DSC Not Registered with DIN' Error in DIR-3 KYC" — https://taxguru.in/company-law/mca-advisory-fix-dsc-not-registered-with-din-error-in-dir-3-kyc.html
  - Describes the exact failure: a director's DSC is technically valid and even linked in the portal's own records, but DIR-3 KYC filing still throws a DSC-not-registered error, and MCA's own advisory admits the fallback is to **raise a manual service complaint** — i.e. the self-service digital process breaks down into a support ticket, defeating the point of the portal.
  - Directors whose registered email and mobile are both inactive (common for a first company set up years ago, KYC now overdue) have no automated recovery path and must also file a manual complaint.

## Cluster 4: Late fee / penalty shock
- Well-documented mechanics (Kanakkupillai, RegisterKaro, IncorpX, corplawupdates fee calculators all converged on the same numbers): ₹100/day per form, **no cap**, since 1 July 2018. A single AOC-4 filed a year late = ₹36,500 in penalty alone. Miss AOC-4 and MGT-7 for three years and penalties alone exceed ₹2,00,000 — before Section 164(2) disqualification risk even kicks in.
- The proliferation of independent "ROC Late Fee Calculator" tools built by multiple unrelated compliance-tech vendors (companyji.com, tradeviser.in, corplawupdates.in, IncorpX all built one) is itself indirect market signal: enough founders/CAs were blindsided by the uncapped daily accrual that a calculator became a lead-gen product category. Nobody builds five independent calculators for a fee structure people already understand.

## Cluster 5: Director disqualification surprise (Section 164(2))
- This is the cluster the secondary report already covers in depth (14,000+ disqualifications in 2025). Primary-voice evidence for this specific cluster was thin in this pass — no first-person Reddit/Twitter account of "I found out I was disqualified when X" was located. What is well-documented (CAclubindia, IncorpX, PatronAccounting, amicuspublico) is the mechanic that makes it a *surprise*: disqualification under 164(2) is triggered by **any one company's** 3-year non-filing, and under Section 167 it automatically vacates the director's seat on **every other company** they hold a directorship in — including ones that are fully compliant. A director can be in good standing at Company A and be silently kicked off Company A's board because Company B (which they may have forgotten they're even listed on, e.g. a friend's defunct startup) defaulted.
- This structural "guilt by association across unrelated companies" mechanic is a strong hook precisely because most named-directors likely don't know it applies to them until they're hit — consistent with the secondary report's framing, and this primary pass did not find any real voice contradicting or softening that read.

## Cluster 6: First-time founder confusion about compliance calendar
- Not independently evidenced with direct quotes in this pass beyond the late-fee-shock material in Cluster 4, which is really the downstream symptom of calendar confusion. Flagged as a gap.

## Signal strength

**Strongest, real-voice-backed:** Cluster 1 (V3 portal bugs/migration chaos). Multiple named, credentialed sources (ICSI President in a formal letter, a named CA/angel investor, a named law firm partner, a named CA-firm founder) plus dated reader comments naming specific unresolved bugs over a 2023–2025 span. This is not a rumor — it is the profession's own trade body formally telling the ministry, on the record, that the portal is blocking legitimate filings.

**Medium:** Cluster 4 (late fee shock) — strong indirect market signal (multiple independent fee-calculator products) plus consistent, converging numbers across independent professional sources, but no raw first-person "I got hit with X" quote captured.

**Weakest / gaps to flag to the team:** Clusters 2, 5, and 6 lack a first-person founder or director quote from this pass. This is a real data gap, not just a search miss — MCA21 audiences (working CAs/CS, incorporated founders) skew toward LinkedIn and closed WhatsApp/Telegram practitioner groups rather than Reddit/Twitter, which are not searchable the way IRCTC or EPFO complaint threads are. If the team wants harder primary evidence for the disqualification-dashboard idea specifically, the next move should be a targeted LinkedIn search for CS/CA posts (not indexed well by general web search) or direct outreach to 2-3 practicing CAs, rather than more Reddit-style queries.

**Net read on the secondary report's director-disqualification-dashboard lead:** This pass neither strongly reinforces nor undermines it with new primary quotes — it's plausible and the underlying mechanic (164(2) + 167 cross-company vacation) is real and surprising, but the loudest, best-evidenced *real-people* pain in this research is actually **Cluster 1, the V3 portal itself breaking basic filing and incorporation flows for weeks at a time**, which the profession has formally and repeatedly gone on record about. Worth the team weighing whether "the portal doesn't do what it says it did" (Cluster 1: filed-but-not-reflected, approved-but-not-updated, DSC-verified-but-rejected) is a stronger wedge than the disqualification angle, since it has by far the most substantiated real-voice evidence found here.
