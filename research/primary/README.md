# Primary research index: real user voices

Complements `research/<Platform>.md` (secondary research: news, CAG, parliamentary sources). These files are primary research: real complaints from Reddit, X/Twitter, YouTube comments, app store reviews, and forums (Team-BHP, CAclubindia, Quora), scraped 2026-08-24.

Method note before you read these: search access was uneven across platforms. Some agents got clean Reddit/X access. Others hit a session-level WebSearch quota wall partway through, or found Reddit and X blocked to direct fetch, and pivoted to live Chrome browsing or forum/press sources instead. Each file states plainly what it could and could not access. Treat "signal was thin" as an honest finding, not a failure to search hard enough.

## What this changes versus the secondary research

Three platforms got a sharper or different lead once real complaints were checked, not just news coverage:

- **UMANG**: the discovery problem (can't find the service) is real but thin on primary quotes. The loud, evidenced complaint is a specific technical failure: EPFO actions inside the app dead-ending after OTP.
- **MCA**: the director-disqualification dashboard is a good idea, but the more evidenced wedge is the MCA21 V3 portal itself failing, backed by named professionals on record, not inferred statistics.
- **National Cyber Crime**: came back the strongest of all ten platforms. If the team wants the single best-evidenced problem statement across the full set, this is it.

## What stayed thin
CPGRAMS has almost no public primary-research footprint. If the team leans toward it, expect to lean on the secondary research (Parliamentary Standing Committee finding) more than real citizen quotes.
