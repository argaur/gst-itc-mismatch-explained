# Build What Moves India

> Team hackathon entry fixing the moment an Indian public-service portal fails the citizen, not redesigning the screen.

## The Problem

**Idea not locked yet.** This is a 4-person team (Gaurav, Harshit, Varun, Kriti) entering "Build What Moves India," a hackathon by Varun Mayya backed by OpenAI: pick one real problem on an Indian public-service website or digital service and build a simpler, clearer, more useful way to solve it. The brief requires fixing the underlying process, not just the UI.

19 candidate ideas are on the table across the team (full list in the project's `CLAUDE.md`). Nearly all of them converge on the same shape: don't rebuild the government portal, fix the moment it fails the citizen — a status that lies, a rejection with no reason, a "disposed" grievance that wasn't actually resolved. That convergence is the team's working thesis, but no single idea has been picked.

## What It Does (once an idea is picked)

Not yet defined. Nothing has been built. Whichever idea the team locks will get its own PRD, rubric score, and Blueprint bootstrap before any code starts.

## Key Decisions

- **Platform locked to Cyber Crime + CPGRAMS (2026-08-25)** — Kriti and Gaurav chose on impact reasoning over the rubric's rank-1/2 picks (Cyber Crime + GST, both 94): Cyber Crime for how deeply tech now touches every person and sector, CPGRAMS as the one mechanism making every department answerable. The Cyber Crime × Harshit overlap flagged 2026-08-24 is still unresolved.
- **Deadline/finale corrected against primary sources (2026-08-24)** — brief page, FAQ page, and the organizer's own video all agree: submission deadline is Aug 28, 2026, 8:00 PM IST (not Aug 27), finale is Sep 12, 2026 in Bengaluru (not Sep 5-6). The 10-platform list itself remains audio-confirmed only, not pixel-verified.
- **Team split to 2-person teams; brief narrowed to 10 named platforms (2026-08-24)** — hackathon organizer rule change. This project now tracks Kriti+Gaurav's work only; Harshit+Varun Malani submit separately.
- **Stays a workspace, not Blueprint, until the idea is locked (2026-08-18)** — running `/prd-create`/`/rubric` before the team converges would build a plan on a guess, and the deadline leaves no slack to redo it. Idea choice is a combined team decision, not Gaurav's alone.
- **RGHS/CGHS framing corrected before going further (2026-08-18)** — the original framing accused government employees as a class of running fake-prescription scams. Research found every documented fraud case (RGHS: 51 hospitals suspended, ~Rs 3 crore in penalties; CGHS: 19 Delhi-NCR hospitals suspended, CBI FIR against 2 Kanpur hospitals) sits with empanelled providers and intermediaries, not employees. Rewritten around the verifiable weakness: a beneficiary can't see what was billed under their own scheme ID.
