## Recommendation rationale

GST wins because the working build can be both honest and demonstrably useful:

- The system has a real, official, current interaction model: DRC-01C lets a taxpayer explain an ITC difference, record a DRC-03 payment ARN, or do both.
- A deterministic reconciliation layer can prove every amount and invoice reference.
- OpenAI is load-bearing for language translation, explanation grouping, uncertainty framing, and the constrained draft narrative.
- The demo can run end to end in under a minute with fully synthetic data and no connection to GSTN.
- The model is not asked to decide tax liability or make payment advice; a qualified professional review boundary is prominent.
