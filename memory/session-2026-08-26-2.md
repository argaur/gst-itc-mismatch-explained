# Session 2026-08-26 (2) — Firecrawl fact-check pass, Harshit-overlap resolved

## What happened

Gaurav asked for a "Firecrawl challenge" on all the research and PRD compilation, pointing to the
published artifact "Ten Portals, Ten Failures" as the compiled findings to check. Dispatched one
Opus 5 agent (per the model-routing hook) to independently re-verify every checkable claim, stat,
and citation across the artifact, `PRD.md`, and `Documentation/research/*.md`, using
`mcp__firecrawl__*` tools.

The agent's report, in order of what mattered most:

- **Wrong law citation.** `PRD.md` and `SECONDARY_RESEARCH.md` cited `BNSS §451/457` for the
  property-release application. Current sections are `§497`/`§503`; `BNSS §451` itself means
  something unrelated. Traced to transcription drift: the primary Reddit source said "Section
  451" without specifying CrPC vs BNSS, and the Stage-C synthesis mislabeled it as BNSS.
- **A live government tool the corpus missed.** MHA/I4C launched the Money Restoration Module
  (`mrm-ncrp.mha.gov.in`) around 20 Aug 2026, five days before the artifact was compiled — a
  tracked refund path for the original fraud complainant. It does not close the gap the PRD
  targets: MRM's login requires an NCRP acknowledgement number, which the uninvolved bystander
  this build is for never has. Reframed everywhere from "no release process exists" to "release
  exists for the complainant, not for the bystander."
- **A hidden strong stat.** The PRD had flagged "2.18%, ₹167 crore" (restoration rate) as
  unverified and told itself not to cite it. Firecrawl confirmed it independently, including on
  I4C's own site. Un-flagged and promoted.
- **An official concession the corpus said didn't exist.** MHA issued revised NCRP/CFCFRMS SOPs
  (reported 27 Jan 2026) explicitly naming "mistaken identity, disputed transactions, or
  insufficient verification" as causes of wrongful freezes. This directly converts the corpus's
  weakest evidence gap (an un-conceded diagnosis) into an official acknowledgment.
- **A legal risk on the core product mechanic.** The RTI-as-discovery step (does any case name me)
  can be refused under RTI Act §8(1)(h) while a case is under investigation — exactly this fact
  pattern. Downgraded that assumption from `[P]` to `[H]` and named the bank-representation-letter
  fallback as a co-equal path, not a rare exception.
- Lower-priority corrections applied to the artifact for corpus integrity: CPGRAMS's "Poor" rating
  threshold confirmed official (was flagged partly-unverified), feedback-disabled closure routes
  corrected from 5 to 8, GST Karnataka episode year corrected from 2024 to 2025, the "9,000
  traders" figure flagged as uncorroborated, mule-account figures refreshed to the June 2026
  numbers.

Ran a second, targeted Firecrawl pass on the specific gaps the first pass flagged: confirmed MRM's
exact tracked states (request ID → URN → SMS/email eligible-amount confirmation), archived the
primary Reddit source's permalink (post and 2.4K/316 engagement confirmed real), and confirmed the
