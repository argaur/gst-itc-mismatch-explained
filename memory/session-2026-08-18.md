## What surprised / what patterns emerged
- Nearly every team idea (Harshit's, Varun's, Kriti's, and now Gaurav's) converges on the same shape: don't redesign the portal's screen, fix the moment it fails the citizen (a status that lies, a rejection with no reason, a refusal that may not be lawful). That convergence is a strong shared thesis for whichever idea the team ends up picking.
- Idea 4 (government employee accountability) had a false premise baked in: CPGRAMS is not actually anonymous, and the Whistleblowers Protection Act 2014 explicitly rules out anonymous disclosures. Worth remembering this class of "I assumed an official channel does X, but it's structurally incapable of X" pattern for other hackathon ideas.
- Gaurav's PMJAY idea overlaps meaningfully with Kriti's existing PM-JAY counter-refusal idea — worth flagging to the team before they lock a pick, so two people don't converge on the same slice unknowingly.
