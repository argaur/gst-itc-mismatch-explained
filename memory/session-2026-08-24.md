## What surprised me / patterns that emerged
- Primary research materially changed the read on three platforms versus secondary research alone: UMANG (an EPFO OTP dead-end inside the app, not pure service-discovery, is the loudest real complaint), MCA (the V3 portal itself failing basic filing, backed by named CS/CA professionals on record, is more evidenced than the director-disqualification angle), and National Cyber Crime (came back as the single strongest-evidenced problem across all 10 platforms — innocent account freezes with no explanation, 2,434-upvote thread).
- CPGRAMS has almost no public primary-research footprint by name — citizens complain about the underlying scheme (EPFO, pension) rather than the meta-portal, which fragments the signal. Worth remembering as a general pattern for any future "aggregator/meta-portal" platform research.
- Several agents lost access to Reddit/X/Quora via direct fetch (403/402 errors) and had to fall back to WebSearch snippets or live Chrome browser navigation via `old.reddit.com/search` — the latter worked reliably when the former didn't. Worth defaulting future Reddit-heavy research agents straight to the Chrome-browser fallback rather than trying WebFetch first.

## Lessons learned
- See `past-mistakes.md`: parallel WebSearch-heavy agent fan-outs can exhaust the session's shared 200-search quota (it's session-scoped, not per-agent) well before all agents finish.
