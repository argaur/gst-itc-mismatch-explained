## What surprised me / patterns that emerged
- All 5 ranked problems came back rated "Strong" evidence — the research passes from the earlier session were thorough enough that there was no need to reach into "moderate" territory to fill out a top 5.
- Reading a live Claude artifact back with `action: "read"` returns the fully rendered/served HTML, including an injected frame-runtime preamble (`<!doctype html><html><head>...`) and closing `</body></html>` wrapper that were never part of the original source file. Republishing that as-is would have double-wrapped the page. Had to strip everything before the original `<title>` tag and after the closing `</script>` before writing it back to a scratchpad file and republishing. Worth remembering as a general pattern any time an artifact is being read back and edited rather than rebuilt from a known local source.
- Rank #1 (Cyber Crime) directly overlaps Harshit's "why is my account frozen" idea from the shared candidate list — flagged clearly in both the artifact's new tab and `project.md` so it isn't missed when the team is deciding.
