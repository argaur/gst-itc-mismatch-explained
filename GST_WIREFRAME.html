  • Confirm whether one ₹3,200 workpaper entry is an accidental duplicate.

What this tool cannot decide

It cannot determine liability, tell you whether to pay or reverse credit, validate a GSTIN, or submit a response.

2. Rules trace source recordsDeterministic code compares the claim workpaper with current and next-period GSTR‑2B rows.
3. OpenAI explains validated findingsThe model gets a small allow-listed payload and returns structured language—not tax calculations.
4. Human reviews the handoffThe user prints a traceable brief. Nothing is filed and no outcome is implied.
